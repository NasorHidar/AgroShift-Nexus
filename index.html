The Challenge

Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data

Climate change makes traditional farming unpredictable. By combining NASA data with collaborative multi-agent reasoning, AgroShift Nexus transforms complex Earth observation and agricultural information into practical farm-level guidance, helping farmers reduce crop loss and strengthen climate-resilient agriculture.

Collaborative Multi-Agent Architecture

Our platform deploys a team of specialized AI agents to analyze farm conditions and recommend optimal crop sequences.

NASA Earth Observation Agent

Analyzes satellite-derived environmental conditions, processing large-scale geospatial data to understand regional climate contexts.

Soil & Farm Agent

Assesses local soil characteristics, moisture levels, and farm topography to determine fundamental land capabilities.

Climate Risk Agent

Identifies volatile climate patterns, predicting potential risks like unseasonal droughts or flash floods based on historical trends.

Crop Intelligence Agent

Evaluates crop suitability and rotation compatibility, ensuring successive crops restore nutrients rather than deplete them.

Economic Preference Agent

Considers market profitability, seed costs, and specific farmer priorities to ensure strategies are financially viable.

Orchestrator Agent

Coordinates all other agents, combines findings, resolves conflicts, and generates the final, actionable crop-rotation strategy.

Powered by NASA Earthdata

AgroShift Nexus relies on cutting-edge space technology to provide grassroots solutions. We integrate critical satellite observations to inform our multi-agent model:

  • SMAP (Soil Moisture Active Passive) Tracks root-zone soil moisture levels to guide irrigation and drought preparedness.
  • GPM/IMERG Provides high-resolution precipitation mapping to forecast rainfall and flood risks.
  • MODIS/VIIRS NDVI Monitors Normalized Difference Vegetation Index to track ongoing crop health and land degradation.
